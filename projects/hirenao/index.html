01 / PROBLEM

Too much repeated work.

Applicants search across sites, rewrite résumés, and track applications separately. I connected those steps so their experience and selected roles carry through to the next task.

02 / DECISIONS

One profile, several uses.

Reuse experience

Career cards feed different résumé versions. Users maintain their accomplishments in one place.

Review AI edits

Show the original, suggested wording, and reason. The extra review step gives users control.

Practice with the résumé

Greenroom can use the chosen résumé, linking interview preparation to the application.

More on tailoring and implementation

Opening Tailor from a job carries its requirements into the résumé review. Saved versions preserve the exact wording used. The app uses Next.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, pgvector, and OpenAI.

03 / WORKFLOW

From a match to an application.

Radar explains why a role matches your résumé and links to the employer’s posting. Tailor brings the job requirements into the résumé review, and Greenroom uses the selected résumé to guide practice.

04 / NEXT STEPS

Where do applicants get stuck?

Watch applicants find a role, prepare their materials, and pick up where they left off. Use those observations to improve the handoffs between Radar, Profile, and Greenroom.
